## Question

When the app reaches broad App Store availability after the invited pilot, who should be able to create or access a Money Buckets account? Decide whether the app remains invitation-gated or supports public signup, and what that means for the intended audience.

## Answer

After the invited pilot, the iOS app can be listed broadly in the App Store while account creation remains invitation-only. A public listing does not imply public signup. Pricing is a separate product decision and is not settled by this access choice.
