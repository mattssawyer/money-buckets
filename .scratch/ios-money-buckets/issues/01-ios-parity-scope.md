## Question

What web tasks and user journeys must the first iOS release support to meet the agreed full-parity direction? Use the current app as the baseline and settle whether parity includes new-user sign-in and onboarding, bank linking, spending-plan setup and editing, Home and transaction review, recurring streams and recurring candidates, payee corrections, Accounts, Investments, Settings, and account deletion.

## Answer

The first iOS release supports all current web workflows end to end: invited-user sign-in and onboarding; connecting, reconnecting, and removing banks; Home balances, spending, guilt-free spending, transaction review and corrections; recurring streams and candidates; creating and editing the spending plan; tracked and shared accounts; investments and net worth; Settings, sign-out, account deletion, and access to Privacy and Terms. These actions are functional, not read-only. Navigation and layout may be designed for iOS; screen-for-screen reproduction is not required.
